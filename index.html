    Voice and settings

    Voice engine

    Neural voice that runs on your device. One-time download of about 90 MB.

    On a Mac, better voices are free: open System Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Manage Voices, and download any marked Enhanced or Premium. Reload this page afterwards.

    Pronunciation fixes

    Whenever the word on the left appears, the voice says what is on the right. Your text on screen does not change.

    Audio file and storage

    Saving audio needs the natural voice. Long documents take a while, because each sentence is generated on your device.

    Shortcuts: Space plays or pauses, the left and right arrows change sentence, [ and ] change speed.
